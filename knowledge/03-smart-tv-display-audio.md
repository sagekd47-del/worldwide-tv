# Smart TV troubleshooting: power, picture, HDMI, network, sound

## TV will not power on
- Confirm wall outlet works with another safe device; inspect remote batteries and physical power button.
- Disconnect external HDMI/USB devices and retry once. Note standby light behavior; exact meanings are model-specific.
- If there is burning smell, sparks, liquid ingress, or unusual heat, stop and seek qualified service. Do not open the TV.

## No picture or black screen
- Confirm TV is on and select the correct input/source.
- Test another HDMI port and known-good cable/source device; change one item at a time.
- Check whether the TV menu/logo appears. If menus show but one source does not, investigate source/cable/input before assuming panel failure.

## No sound
- Check mute, volume, sound output selection, and whether TV speakers or external ARC/eARC/Bluetooth device is selected.
- Test another channel/app/input. Reconnect external audio equipment and confirm compatible HDMI ARC/eARC port and settings using the exact manual.

## Wi-Fi or streaming problems
- Test another app and another device on the same Wi-Fi.
- Check TV date/time, signal strength, router access, and app/service status.
- Restart the TV normally. Avoid factory reset until account sign-ins and settings are known.

## Important
TV model and firmware are essential for menu paths and firmware guidance. Never recommend installing firmware from unofficial mirrors or using a firmware package for a similar-looking model.
