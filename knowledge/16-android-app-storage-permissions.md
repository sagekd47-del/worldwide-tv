# Android app permissions, cache, and crashes

## Symptoms
An app closes, freezes, cannot access camera/location/files, or reports insufficient storage.

## Safe checks
- Restart the phone and update the app from its official store.
- Review the app's permissions in system Settings and grant only permissions needed for the feature.
- Check free storage.
- Clear cache where the Android version provides that option; clearing storage/data can remove local app data and sign-in state.
- Check whether the issue affects one app or many apps.
- Reinstall only after confirming the account can sign back in and important app data is backed up.

## Cautions
Avoid downloading APKs from unknown websites or granting Accessibility/Device Admin permissions to untrusted apps.
