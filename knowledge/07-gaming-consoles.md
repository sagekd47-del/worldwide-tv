# Gaming console troubleshooting

## Console will not start
- Check power outlet, supplied power cable/adapter, ventilation, and front indicator behavior.
- Disconnect accessories and external storage, then retry once.
- Do not open the power supply or console while connected to mains power.

## Overheating or shutdown
- Ensure vents are unobstructed and the console is on a hard, open surface.
- Allow it to cool; do not use liquids or compressed air in a way prohibited by the exact manufacturer's instructions.
- Repeated thermal shutdowns require model-specific service diagnosis.

## Network, downloads, or online play
- Check service status, NAT/network conditions, account restrictions, and whether other devices have internet.
- Record exact error code, console model, system software, game, and network type.

## Controller pairing
- Charge or replace batteries, use the model-specific pairing procedure, and test with another cable/USB port if supported.
