# Laptop and desktop troubleshooting: boot, power, storage, display

## No power
- Check outlet, power cable/adapter seating, dock, and external peripherals. Look for indicator lights and fan behavior.
- Do not open the power supply or work on live mains equipment.

## No boot or startup error
- Capture the exact error text/code and manufacturer diagnostic code; record recent hardware/software changes.
- Disconnect nonessential USB devices and retry. Use the manufacturer's built-in diagnostics and model-specific support instructions.
- Do not change BIOS/UEFI storage mode, Secure Boot, TPM, or boot order without understanding the current configuration and recovery-key implications.

## Slow computer
- Check free storage, startup apps, task manager/resource use, pending updates, malware protection status, and whether one process is consuming resources.
- Back up important files before disk repair, OS reset, or reinstall.

## Display issue
- Increase brightness, verify display output mode, test an external monitor/cable if available, and note whether the BIOS/logo appears.
- A working external monitor does not alone prove the internal panel is faulty; cable, backlight, GPU, and configuration may also be involved.

## Storage warning
If a drive clicks, disappears intermittently, reports SMART failure, or becomes extremely slow, prioritize backing up important data and avoid repeated stress tests. Seek data recovery advice if the data is valuable.
