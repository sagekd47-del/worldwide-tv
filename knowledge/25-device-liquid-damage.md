# Liquid exposure and electronic device safety

## Symptoms
Device was splashed, submerged, exposed to rain, or has moisture visible in ports.

## Immediate safe response
- Disconnect power and accessories if this can be done safely; do not touch wet mains plugs or power strips.
- Power the device off if safe. Do not repeatedly try to turn it on.
- Remove external accessories and dry the outside with a lint-free cloth.
- Follow the manufacturer's model-specific liquid exposure guidance and arrange professional inspection, especially after submersion.

## Do not
- Do not charge a wet device.
- Do not use a hair dryer, oven, heater, or compressed air to force-dry it.
- Do not insert objects into ports or open sealed devices unless qualified.
- Do not assume that a device working immediately after exposure is free of corrosion risk.
