# Printer troubleshooting: paper, print quality, connectivity

## Printer offline or not detected
- Confirm printer power and ready state; inspect the displayed error.
- Verify USB/Wi-Fi/Ethernet connection and that computer and printer are on the intended network.
- Check selected printer and queue; clear stuck jobs only after recording them if needed.
- Reinstall drivers only from the manufacturer's official support page for the exact model and operating system.

## Paper jam
- Follow the exact model's access-panel instructions; power off if the manual says to.
- Pull paper in the recommended direction, avoid tearing, and do not force rollers or use sharp tools.

## Poor print quality
- Check paper type/size, ink or toner level, protective seals, and correct media settings.
- Use the printer's built-in nozzle check, cleaning, or calibration only as described in its manual; repeated cleaning may consume ink.
