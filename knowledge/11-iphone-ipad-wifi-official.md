# iPhone and iPad Wi-Fi troubleshooting

## Evidence to collect
Device model, iOS/iPadOS version, exact Wi-Fi status, whether other devices connect to the same router, whether the problem occurs on another Wi-Fi network, and any VPN or management profile context.

## Safe diagnostic sequence
1. Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled and the intended network is selected; check Airplane Mode.
2. Compare another device on the same network. If several devices cannot get online, investigate router/modem/ISP rather than assuming an iPhone fault.
3. Restart the iPhone/iPad and router/modem when appropriate.
4. Try another supported Wi-Fi band/network if available.
5. Check whether a VPN or security app is affecting connectivity; do not remove work/school profiles without administrator advice.
6. Reset Network Settings only after simpler checks. This removes saved Wi-Fi networks/passwords and resets cellular, VPN and APN settings.
