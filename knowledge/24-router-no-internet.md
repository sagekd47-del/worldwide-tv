# Router has Wi-Fi but no internet

## Symptoms
Devices connect to the Wi-Fi network but report no internet access.

## Safe checks
- Check the router's WAN/Internet indicator and the modem/ONT status lights; record their labels and colors.
- Restart modem/ONT and router in the order recommended by the ISP, allowing each to reconnect fully.
- Check whether the ISP reports an outage or account/service issue.
- If possible, test a wired connection directly to the router using a known-working cable.
- Do not factory-reset the router unless the ISP or administrator has the configuration details needed to restore service.

## Cautions
Do not share router admin passwords or Wi-Fi passwords publicly. Avoid changing WAN, VLAN, or authentication settings without ISP guidance.
