# Low storage and disk space

## Symptoms
Warnings that storage is full, apps cannot update, files cannot save, or a computer/phone becomes slow.

## Safe checks
- Check storage usage in the operating system settings and identify which category is consuming space.
- Empty the recycle bin only after checking its contents.
- Remove apps and downloads that the owner recognises and no longer needs.
- Move personal files to a verified backup before deleting local copies.
- Use built-in Storage Sense or device storage recommendations where available.

## Cautions
Never delete unknown system folders, recovery partitions, or application data folders manually. Do not promise that a cleaner app will improve performance.
