# USB device not detected

## Symptoms
A flash drive, phone, keyboard, mouse, or external disk does not appear when connected.

## Safe checks
- Try a different known-working USB port and cable; some cables support charging only, not data.
- Connect directly to the computer rather than through an unpowered hub.
- Test the accessory on another compatible device to isolate the accessory or host.
- For external drives, check Disk Management or the operating system's storage utility without formatting or initialising the disk.
- For phones, unlock the phone and choose the correct USB mode if prompted.

## Cautions
Do not format a drive that contains needed data. Safely eject storage before disconnecting when possible.
