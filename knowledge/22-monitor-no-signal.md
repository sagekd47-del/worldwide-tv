# Monitor displays No Signal

## Symptoms
Monitor powers on but reports No Signal or enters standby.

## Safe checks
- Confirm the computer is powered on and not asleep.
- Select the correct monitor input (HDMI, DisplayPort, USB-C, etc.).
- Reseat both ends of the video cable and test another compatible cable or port.
- If a desktop has a separate graphics card, use the graphics card's video output rather than the motherboard output when appropriate for the configuration.
- Test the monitor with another known-working source, or the computer with another display.

## Cautions
Do not force connectors or open the monitor. Record any diagnostic LED or beep pattern from the computer.
