# Camera or microphone not working

## Symptoms
Camera is black, microphone records no sound, or apps say no device is available.

## Safe checks
- Test the camera/microphone in a second trusted app to isolate an app-specific issue.
- Check privacy permissions for the app and operating system.
- Check physical privacy shutters, mute switches, keyboard function keys, and selected input/output devices.
- Restart the device and install official system and driver updates.
- On computers, check Device Manager or system hardware settings for a disabled or error-marked device.

## Cautions
Do not install driver bundles from unofficial download sites. Respect camera and microphone privacy.
