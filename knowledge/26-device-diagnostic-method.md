# General device diagnostic method

## Evidence-first workflow
1. Identify exact device category, manufacturer, model, operating system/firmware, and approximate age.
2. Capture the full error text/code and when it occurs.
3. Ask what changed immediately before the fault: update, drop, liquid, new accessory, settings change, power event, or new app.
4. Determine scope: one app, one device, all devices on a network, or one power source.
5. Reproduce the symptom safely and change one variable at a time.
6. Start with reversible, non-invasive checks and explain expected outcomes.
7. Check manufacturer documentation for model-specific procedures before recommending reset, firmware, parts, or repair.
8. Record what was tested and the result; do not label a possible cause as confirmed without a test.

## Escalation
Stop user-level troubleshooting when there is smoke, sparks, burning smell, liquid ingress, severe heat, swollen battery, damaged mains cable, or suspected internal power-supply fault. Refer to qualified service.
