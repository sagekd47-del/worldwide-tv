# Android SIM and mobile network checks

## Symptoms
No SIM, no service, emergency calls only, mobile data unavailable, or calls failing.

## Safe checks
- Confirm Airplane mode is off and restart the phone.
- Check whether the SIM is detected in Settings and whether the SIM is active with the carrier.
- If safe and supported, power off before reseating a physical SIM; do not force a tray.
- Test the SIM in another compatible unlocked phone, or test a known-working SIM in this phone, to isolate SIM versus handset.
- Check network coverage, account status, data allowance, and carrier outage information.
- Reset network settings only after explaining that saved Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pairings may be removed.

## Cautions
Do not publish or share the full SIM number, account PIN, or one-time codes.
