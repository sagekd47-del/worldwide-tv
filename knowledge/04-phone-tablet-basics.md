# Smartphone and tablet troubleshooting

## Slow performance or app crashes
- Record device model, OS version, app name/version, available storage, and whether the issue began after an update.
- Restart once; check free storage and whether the problem is limited to one app.
- Update apps/OS only through official update channels and with adequate battery/power and backup.
- Clear an app cache only when the OS provides that option; explain that clearing app data may remove local information.

## Battery drains quickly or device overheats
- Check battery usage by app, signal conditions, screen brightness, background activity, and recent updates.
- Stop gaming/charging and let the device cool if unusually hot. Do not put it in a refrigerator/freezer.
- If battery is swollen, leaking, hissing, or smells unusual, stop using/charging and seek professional service.

## Charging problems
- Inspect cable/adapter for damage and try a known-good compatible charger and outlet.
- Check port for visible lint without inserting metal tools or liquids. If wet, follow the manufacturer's liquid-exposure guidance and do not charge until safe.
- Charging speed and wattage depend on exact model and supported charger standards; do not guess.

## Connectivity
- Compare Wi-Fi/mobile data/Bluetooth with another device and note whether only one network or accessory is affected.
- Verify airplane mode, SIM/eSIM status, account/service coverage, and network settings before considering a network reset.

## Data safety
Back up before factory reset, bootloader unlocking, firmware flashing, or repair actions that may erase data. Never request passwords, one-time codes, or account recovery secrets from the user.
