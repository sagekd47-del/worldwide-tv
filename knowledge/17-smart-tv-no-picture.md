# Smart TV has sound but no picture

## Symptoms
TV powers on and audio works, but the screen is black, very dim, or has no picture.

## Safe checks
- Press Menu/Home and check whether the TV's own menu is visible.
- Confirm the selected HDMI/input source and test another source or known-working HDMI cable.
- Disconnect external HDMI devices and restart the TV using its documented power cycle procedure.
- Check picture settings such as screen-off, energy saving, or picture mode.
- If the TV menu is visible but one HDMI source is not, investigate that source device and cable first.

## Cautions
If no menu is visible, the screen flashes, or a faint image is visible only with a flashlight, internal panel/backlight/power faults are possible. Do not open the TV; hazardous voltages may remain inside after unplugging. Seek qualified TV service.
