# Router and Wi-Fi troubleshooting

## No internet on all connected devices
- Check the ISP modem/ONT and router power/status lights against the exact model manual; light meanings vary by model.
- Check for an ISP outage or account/service issue before resetting equipment.
- Restart modem/ONT and router using the provider's recommended sequence. Avoid factory reset unless credentials and ISP configuration are available.

## One device cannot connect
- Verify the password, airplane mode, Wi-Fi toggle, date/time, and whether the device is blocked by access control or parental controls.
- Compare 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz availability; range and compatibility differ.
- Forget/rejoin the network only after confirming the correct password.

## Weak signal or intermittent connection
- Test near the router to distinguish coverage from ISP issues.
- Move router to an open, central, elevated location; avoid cabinets and large metal obstructions.
- Check whether the problem occurs at particular times or only on one band/channel.
- Record router model, firmware, ISP, connection type, and affected rooms before changing advanced settings.

## Security
Never share Wi-Fi passwords publicly. Do not disable encryption or firewall as a routine troubleshooting step. Use WPA2-AES or WPA3 where supported and a unique administrator password. Consult the router maker's exact manual for settings.
